# PowerBI-Social-Media-Dashboard
Social Media Performance Dashboard created by using PowerBI.

## 📄 Project overview 
This project focuses on analyzing social media performance across multiple platforms to understand audience engagement, content effectiveness, and growth trends.
The analysis helps identify which platforms, content types, and posting times perform best and supports data-driven decision-making for social media strategy.
The dashboard is divided into three pages to provide a clear summary, detailed analysis, and time-based and location-based insights.

## 🎯 Objective of the project
The main objectives of this project are:
* To analyze overall social media performance.
* To measure engagement across different platforms.
* To identify top-performing content and posts.
* To study trends over time (daily / monthly).
* To provide insights for improving content strategy.

## 📊 Key KPIs
* Total Posts
* Total Engagement
* Average Engagement Rate
* Total Views
* Total Likes
* Total Comments
* Total Shares
* Click-Through Rate (CTR)
* Engagement per Post

## 📃 Dashboard Pages
### 📌 Executive Summary : 
* Shows overall social media performance at a high level.
* Displays total posts, total engagement, views, likes, comments, and shares.
* Shows daily engagement trends over time.
* Provides engagement distribution by region.
* Helps stakeholders quickly understand overall performance .

### 📈 Platform and content deep dive : 
* Analyzes engagement across social media platforms (Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, X.com).
* Shows engagement composition using likes and shares.
* Analyzes performance by post type and content type.
* Identifies top-performing hashtags.
* Displays top-performing posts by likes and platform.

### 🌍 Temporal & Geo Performance :
* Analyzes engagement trends by time and day.
* Shows post frequency across different months and regions.
* Displays geographic engagement distribution using maps.
* Identifies best-performing time periods and locations.

## 🔍 Key Insights :
* Instagram and YouTube show the highest engagement.
* Video and live stream content receive more views and clicks.
* Specific hashtags generate higher engagement and performance scores.
* Audience engagement is higher during evening hours and mid-week.
* Some regions consistently perform better than others.
* Consistent posting improves engagement and audience retention.

Power BI – Data cleaning, modeling, and dashboard creation
